Do more with more — the same or more people, producing broader output. Quality is
non-negotiable, so agents audit continuously rather than at review gates. And
provider independence keeps a single vendor from becoming a single point of
failure.

## What changes in practice

The unit of planning stops being the sprint and becomes the run. A run carries
its own audit trail, which means review moves from a meeting to a diff.
