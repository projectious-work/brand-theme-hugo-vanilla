---
title: "Why agent-first?"
url: "https://projectious-work.github.io/brand-theme-hugo-vanilla/blog/why-agent-first/"

---


Do more with more: augment people's strengths so the same or more people produce more, at higher quality, for broader markets.

Quality stays non-negotiable, because agents audit continuously — you ship the same software with continuous review instead of a periodic one.

Provider independence matters too. Agnostic, self-hosted where it counts, so no single AI vendor becomes a single point of failure.
