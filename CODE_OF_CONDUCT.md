# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and assume good intent while discussing evidence.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Respect privacy, attribution and intellectual-property obligations.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes and focus on improving the work.
- Keep project spaces relevant and safe for participation.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact
method on the projectious.work organization profile. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, issue warnings, restrict participation or ban contributors when necessary.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical and without retaliation.

This policy applies to repository interactions and to public spaces where someone
represents the project.
